# Sources of the cutscenes and documentary captions

Every event, line and number the cinema shows comes from the records below. The films 노량: 죽음의 바다 (2023), 명량 (2014)
and 한산 (2022) are references for camera, light and pace only; nothing they invented is shown as fact. Grades follow the
maneuver research (A: the commander's own report or the court annals; B: near-contemporary Korean or Ming accounts;
C: later compilations and modern reference works; D: unsourced).

## Maneuver captions without a historical claim

Raking fire, crossfire, boarding, a magazine explosion, a fleet wavering or breaking, the line, the column (with the
Busan report's "長蛇突前") and the wedge explain what the game is doing; they cite nothing unless a record says it.
