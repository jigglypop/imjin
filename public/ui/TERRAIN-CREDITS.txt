IMJIN terrain data

Mapzen Terrain Tiles, accessed from the Registry of Open Data on AWS on 2026-10-11.
https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/
https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md

Global SRTM and GMTED2010 elevations: U.S. Geological Survey.
Global ETOPO1 elevations: U.S. Department of Commerce, NOAA, NESDIS, NCEI.
These global U.S. government datasets are public-domain data. Source resolution
varies; Terrain Tiles does not identify the contributing dataset for each pixel.
This display is for a game and is not suitable for navigation or surveying.

Assets and modifications:
- korea_dem.webp: Terrarium elevation tiles, stitched and despiked by
  scripts/fetch-dem.mjs. Retains encoded height data rather than a painted map.
- grand_map.webp: shaded fallback image built by scripts/build-grand-map.mjs.
- grand_relief.bin: the same Mercator frame and DEM override, resampled to
  257 x 140 unsigned little-endian metre heights. Sea heights are clamped to zero.
  Rebuild: node scripts/build-grand-map.mjs --relief-only
- The 3D campaign view exaggerates vertical heights 20 times for readability.
  Port coordinates are approximate campaign markers, not measured harbour positions.

The geographic frame and generated grid metadata are committed as
src/ui/grand/mapFrame.json and src/ui/grand/reliefMeta.json.
