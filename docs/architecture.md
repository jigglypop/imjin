# Architecture

## Screens and the battle
- `src/App.tsx` screens (store `screen`; every screen below the menu is a lazy chunk, `screenChunks` in `src/router.ts`), URL hooks, the battle `<Canvas>`; `src/state/store.ts` the UI snapshot the engine publishes every 0.15 s.
- `src/game/Engine.ts` owns a battle: init/stage (sky, terrain, ships, ocean, clouds, crew), the fixed-step sim loop with speed and auto fast-forward, event dispatch to sound/fx/crew, snapshot publish. `quality.ts` equipment tiers (high PC / medium tablet / low phone) and run-time levels; `adaptive.ts` the fps controller; `device.ts` touch/phone detection.
- `src/cinema/` cutscenes and the documentary mode, rendered from the running battle: `scenes/` (openings of Noryang, Hansan, Myeongnyang, the Noryang closing) and `beats.ts` (captions on sim events) are written from the records in `SOURCES.md`; `rig.ts` frames shots, `CutscenePlayer.ts` plays a scene, `Cinema.ts` drives camera, speed, sound and the film grade (`grade.ts`); `src/ui/CinemaOverlay.tsx` letterbox, captions and labels. Online it is captions only.

## Simulation (`src/sim`, shared with `server/`)
- `battle.ts` (ships, AI think, guns, projectiles, boarding), `catalog.ts` (SHIP_SPECS, GUN_SPECS), `scenarios.ts` (9 historical battles), `conquest.ts` + `maps.ts` + `strategy.ts` (capture-point skirmish and the computer strategist), `balance.ts`, `types.ts` (Ship, BattleEvent).
- Depth rules are versioned in `depth.ts`. depth-v2 is on for historical battles, conquest against the computer and the campaign's 3D battles; ranked and online conquest keep the classic rules, step for step. `morale.ts` (nerve, waver, rout, rally, the drum, lost leaders), `tactics.ts` (raking, crossfire, cohesion), `fire.ts` (fire jumping downwind), `maneuvers.ts` (squadron maneuvers as orders, station keeping, no fire through friends, rule of the road, command radius, breach cut-offs, routs to open water) and `tactician.ts` (the computer's commanders by temper).

## Ships, effects, environment
- `src/ships/`: ShipRenderer (instanced ships, 3 LODs, atlas material), ShipViews (LOD pick, wave motion, sinking pose), decks.ts (deck heights, crew stations), `build/` (procedural ships from modular parts and per-faction gpt-image atlases; `scripts/ship-textures.mjs patch` swaps single cells; `bake.ts` bakes occlusion and gun soot into the vertices), `weather.ts` (film-prop weathering), `anchors.ts` (three-free gun ports, decks, flags per model; the sim's `Battle.muzzle` and `variantFor` read it too).
- `src/fx/`: Effects (muzzle, projectiles, hits, debris), ParticleLayer (CPU particles, TSL sprites), Crew (GPU-skinned baked crews; `Crew.dispose`, not just removing the group, frees its props), Lanterns. Smoke, fire and spray shade from one noise texture (`particleNoise.ts`).
- `src/ocean/`: FFT (WebGPU compute only) or the Gerstner fallback. `src/render/`: `atmosphereModel.ts` integrates a physical atmosphere on the CPU at load for the battle's sun (one sky view that the background, the fog and every haze read, an environment map with the clouds); `skyDome.ts` draws it with the sun, or the moon and stars; `Clouds.ts` raymarched from a weather map shared with the sea (`src/ocean/seaFields.ts`: gusts, wave groups, cloud shadows), accumulated over jittered frames.
- `src/terrain/`: worker heightmap, vegetation, structures. Terrain is drawn in culled tiles; each forest ring is a GPU-driven grid of tree cells, and `Vegetation.gather` lists on the CPU only the land cells near the screen, so the vertex shader never runs for sea or off-screen cells.

## Audio (`src/audio`)
Sound.ts (context, buses, settings), battlefield.ts (sim events to voices: range model, broadsides, far booms, ducking, rate limits), samples.ts (the decoded `public/audio/*.mp3` bank, credits in `public/audio/CREDITS.md`), voices.ts (synthesised fallbacks until the bank decodes), ambience.ts, music.ts (drone and far drums, no melody), ui.ts, mix.ts.

## Campaign
- `src/sim/grand/*` the faction campaign (캠페인), pure TS: regions, economy (through `TUNING` and the competitive profile in `competitive.ts`), orders, turn, ai, autoresolve, march (`Grand.march = 'tw-v1'`: distance per turn, fleets meeting where lanes cross, zones of control, reinforcements, blockades, winter; older saves keep one lane a turn), bridge (the hand-off to a 3D region battle), coast (each region's battle map on the terrain of the nearest historical battle), spawn, report, events (dated historical events; a turn is a season, so 1597 is turns 20-22), replay, roster. `src/campaign/grand.ts` is its store (`imjin.grand.v1`).
- UI: `src/ui/GrandScreen.tsx` over the prop-driven kit in `src/ui/grand/*` (`adapt.ts` maps the store onto view models). A meeting played in 3D is a `grand` launch (`launch.ts`) and `Engine` hands the per-ship outcome back through `finishGrandBattle`.
- `src/campaign/campaign.ts` the linear 1592 campaign; `src/select/SelectScene.ts` the 3D ink map of the south coast.

## Multiplayer
`src/net/` + `server/`: authoritative Node server (`main.ts` rooms, sessions, quick match, tick; `battles.ts` conquest and historical-duel builders; `terrain.ts` heightmaps in worker threads; `ranked.ts` the ranked journal), binary snapshots, protocol in `protocol.ts`. Lobby for conquest maps and historical duels, quick match (the computer fills after 20 s), reconnect with a per-tab session token (seat kept 60 s), the approach skipped at up to 32x until first contact. UI in `src/ui/OnlinePanel.tsx`, `OnlineOverlay.tsx`.

## Accounts
- Server: `server/accounts.ts` the store (node:sqlite at `ACCOUNTS_DB`, default `data/accounts.db` beside the bundle: users, hashed sessions with a sliding 90-day expiry, one JSON profile per user with a revision, an append-only currency ledger, one-time claims; `updateProfile` runs a change with the ledger and claims in one transaction; a dated `VACUUM INTO` copy at start and daily, seven kept), `password.ts` (scrypt, parameters in the hash), `api.ts` (the `/api/` router beside `/health` and `/ws`: CORS, 16 KB JSON, Bearer sessions, `{ error: { code, text } }`, `Limiter`; `user()` routes get the signed-in user), `auth.ts` (guest, sign-up in place, log-in, log-out, `/api/me`, nickname, password, link-ranked). The shapes and rules both sides share are in `src/account/wire.ts`.
- A hello with `session` plays with the signed-in user's ranked account (made and linked on the first visit, a guest keeps its browser's); without one the recovery code works as before.
- Client: `src/account/` (`session.ts` the token in `imjin.session.v1`, `api.ts`, `store.ts` with `ensureAccount()` for the first thing worth keeping); the menu's account panel is `src/ui/AccountPanel.tsx`. `scripts/test-accounts.mjs` covers it end to end.

## Phone memory, in detail
- What the low tier loads is decided in `quality.ts`: no 2048 px ship models or normal/roughness maps, the smaller computed sky (`skySize`), 1k ground textures (`scripts/build-terrain-tex.mjs`), only the ship kinds the battle uses. A battle must not make the renderer create new sky textures (`loadSky` reuses them).
- The WebGL2 backend is corrected in `src/render/webglBackend.ts`: instanced matrices come from vertex attributes (batches of one model share a program) and the vertex-array cache drops what a destroyed attribute used. `freeAfterDraw` (`src/render/dispose.ts`) detaches the CPU copy of terrain and ship vertex data once drawn. A node whose texture flips every frame (the wake map) leaves dead bind groups in the texture it is not showing when its material dies: `WakeMap.release` purges them.
- A uniform many materials read (the `atmosphere` ones) belongs in `renderGroup`: in the default per-object group each draw updates its own copy, which cost Noryang a quarter of its frame rate.
